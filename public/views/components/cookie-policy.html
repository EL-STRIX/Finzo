Cookie Policy

Finzo Banking Management System • Effective Date: April 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the Finzo Banking Management System (“Finzo”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you access our digital banking platform.

We prioritize user privacy and data security. We do not use third-party marketing or cross-site tracking cookies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by your web browser when you visit a website. They help recognize your browser session, maintain secure authentication, remember preferences, and ensure banking transactions operate without disruption.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary & Authentication Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of our enterprise banking application. They enable you to securely log in, verify your identity, navigate between pages, and access authenticated banking dashboards. Without these cookies, services like transfers, balance lookups, and session management cannot function.

  • Session Identifiers (PHPSESSID): Maintains your secure active login session with encrypted authentication tokens.
  • CSRF Protection Tokens: Protects against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks during state-changing financial transactions.
  • Device Verification Tokens: Helps detect suspicious logins from unrecognized browsers or devices.

Security & Integrity Cookies

Used to enforce rate-limiting, detect brute-force attempts, prevent automated bot attacks, and protect customer account integrity.

Preference & Functional Cookies

These cookies allow our platform to remember choices you make (such as UI display preferences, table pagination sizes, and filter selections) to provide an enhanced personal banking experience.

3. Third-Party Cookies & Advertising

Finzo adheres to strict enterprise privacy standards. We do not sell, rent, or lease cookie telemetry to advertising networks, commercial brokers, or behavioral tracking entities. All telemetry is strictly confined to system performance and security governance.

4. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can control and manage cookies through your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, manage, delete, and block cookies for specific domains.

Note: Because cookies are vital for verifying authenticated sessions and executing secure fund transfers, disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from accessing the Finzo banking portal.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect technological, operational, or legal requirements. Material modifications will be reflected with an updated effective date.
