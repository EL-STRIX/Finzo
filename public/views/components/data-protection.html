Data Protection & Governance

Finzo Banking Management System • Enterprise Security & Privacy Governance

Finzo Banking Management System (“Finzo”) adheres to rigorous enterprise-grade data protection principles. This Charter outlines how we safeguard financial records, uphold regulatory data compliance, and protect our customers against unauthorized data access.

1. Core Data Protection Principles

  • Data Minimization: We only collect, process, and retain information strictly necessary for executing lawful banking services and verifying customer identities.
  • Cryptographic Security: All customer data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 standards; data in transit is protected via TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Strict Purpose Limitation: Financial data is processed exclusively for account maintenance, fraud prevention, regulatory compliance, and transaction settlement.
  • Zero Data Monetization: We never sell, monetize, or trade customer transaction history or personal identifiers to third-party commercial data brokers.

2. Access Control & Segregation of Duties

Finzo enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and the Principle of Least Privilege across all administrative and staff endpoints:

  • Staff access to customer profiles requires active duty verification and is subject to automated audit logging.
  • Database queries are parameterized through PDO prepared statements, preventing SQL injection and unauthorized data leakage.
  • Administrative privileges are strictly partitioned from financial settlement and user credential storage.

3. Data Subject Rights

In accordance with applicable privacy frameworks (including GDPR principles), customers retain comprehensive rights concerning their stored information:

  • Right to Access: Review complete profile information and request comprehensive transaction history exports at any time from your dashboard.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of obsolete or inaccurate personal details.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request temporary restriction of account activity during dispute resolution.
  • Data Retention & Audit Trails: Banking records are retained in compliance with statutory financial retention laws and securely archived against alteration.

4. Incident Response & Breach Notification

Finzo operates 24/7 automated telemetry and intrusion detection systems. In the event of a verified data incident impacting customer records, our incident response protocol mandates prompt customer notification and regulatory reporting in compliance with statutory deadlines.
