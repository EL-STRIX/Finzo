Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Counter-Terrorist Financing

Finzo Banking Management System • Regulatory Compliance & Financial Integrity

Finzo Banking Management System (“Finzo”) is committed to the highest standards of financial integrity, statutory compliance, and crime prevention. Our Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF) Framework establishes robust protocols to prevent the misuse of our banking infrastructure.

1. Regulatory Standards & Governance

Finzo adheres to international standards set forth by the Financial Action Task Force (FATF), regional AML directives, and applicable national banking regulations. Our compliance controls are structured to ensure every customer onboarding and fund transfer conforms with legal mandates.

2. Customer Due Diligence (CDD) & KYC

We enforce a strict Know Your Customer (KYC) policy across all customer accounts:

  • Identity Verification: Full legal name, date of birth, government-issued identification, contact credentials, and residential verification prior to account activation.
  • Beneficiary Validation: Algorithmic and database checks on recipient accounts to ensure routing legitimacy.
  • Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD): Elevated review protocols for high-value accounts, politically exposed persons (PEPs), and atypical transaction profiles.

3. Automated Transaction Monitoring & Risk Scoring

Our ACID-compliant transaction pipeline integrates continuous monitoring to detect anomalies and suspicious behavior in real time:

  • Velocity & Volume Checks: Automated alerts on rapid successive transfers, structural layering attempts, or sudden deviations from typical account behavior.
  • Sanction Screening: Automatic cross-referencing against global sanctions lists and embargo registries.
  • Lockout & Suspension Mechanisms: Automated temporary holds on accounts demonstrating indicators of compromise or illicit funding.

4. Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR)

When transaction monitoring reveals unresolvable discrepancies or reasonable grounds to suspect illegal activity, Finzo maintains mandatory reporting protocols to relevant Financial Intelligence Units (FIU) in accordance with applicable legal frameworks.
