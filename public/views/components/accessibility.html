Accessibility Statement

Finzo Banking Management System • Conformance & Inclusivity Commitment

Finzo Banking Management System (“Finzo”) is dedicated to ensuring digital banking accessibility for all customers, including individuals with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience and apply relevant accessibility standards across our digital ecosystem.

1. Conformance Standard

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. These guidelines define best practices for making web content accessible to individuals with a broad range of sensory, cognitive, and motor abilities.

2. Key Accessibility Features

  • Keyboard Navigation: All critical workflows—including authentication, balance inspection, fund transfers, and form submissions—are fully operable using keyboard-only input.
  • High Contrast & Visual Hierarchy: Our Design System enforces strong contrast ratios (meeting or exceeding 4.5:1 for standard text) and avoids color as the sole indicator of system state.
  • Screen Reader Optimization: Forms, inputs, status badges, dynamic modals, and alerts utilize appropriate WAI-ARIA labels, landmarks, and semantic HTML elements.
  • Focus Management: Interactive elements feature clear, visible focus rings and logical tab order to assist navigation.
  • Scalable Typography: Layouts adapt gracefully to text resizing and zooming up to 200% without loss of content or functionality.

3. Assistive Technology Compatibility

Our platform is engineered and routinely verified for compatibility with modern assistive technologies, including popular screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, and TalkBack) and major web browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

4. Continuous Evaluation & Maintenance

Accessibility is an ongoing engineering priority. Our development team conducts automated linting, manual keyboard walkthroughs, and periodic assistive technology evaluations during our continuous integration and deployment pipelines.
