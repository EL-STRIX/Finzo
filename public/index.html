Frequently Asked Questions

How do I open an account?

Opening an account is seamless. Simply click "Open Account" in the navigation, provide your fundamental identity details, complete our rapid KYC verification, and access your dashboard immediately.

Is my data secure with Finzo?

Yes. We utilize AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. All sensitive operations require secure session validation.

How quickly are transfers processed?

Internal transfers between Finzo accounts are processed in real-time, executing immediately with absolute transactional consistency.

How is interest calculated on savings accounts?

Interest is calculated based on your average daily balance and automatically credited to your account at the end of every 30-day period.

Can I access my account statements online?

Absolutely. Full transaction histories are maintained indefinitely and can be exported as secure PDF documents from your dashboard at any time.
