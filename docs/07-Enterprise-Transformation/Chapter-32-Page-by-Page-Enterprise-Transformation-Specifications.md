# Part 1 — Public Website Transformation Specifications

## Purpose

This part defines the transformation specifications for every public-facing page of the Finzo Banking Management System. The objective is to transform the public website into a professional, trustworthy, and enterprise-grade digital banking platform while maintaining FEPS compliance.

Unlike previous chapters, this chapter specifies **what must be transformed** rather than defining design standards or audit methodologies.

---

## Scope

This specification applies to all public pages, including:

- Home Page
- About Page
- Services Page
- Contact Page
- FAQ
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Error Pages
- Other Public Information Pages

Every public page shall follow the standards established throughout FEPS.

---

## Transformation Objectives

Every public page shall:

- Present a professional banking identity.
- Build customer trust.
- Improve information clarity.
- Strengthen brand consistency.
- Encourage user engagement.
- Maintain enterprise-quality visual design.
- Support accessibility and responsiveness.
- Preserve existing functionality.

---

## Home Page

Review and improve:

- Hero Section
- Value Proposition
- Banking Services Overview
- Trust Indicators
- Feature Highlights
- Security Information
- Customer Benefits
- Calls-to-Action
- Footer Content

The homepage should immediately communicate professionalism, security, and reliability.

---

## About Page

Verify:

- Company Introduction
- Mission
- Vision
- Core Values
- Banking Philosophy
- Customer Commitment
- Brand Story
- Leadership Information (if applicable)

The page should reinforce credibility and transparency.

---

## Services Page

Review:

- Service Categories
- Product Descriptions
- Benefits
- Feature Presentation
- Visual Organization
- Service Comparison
- Action Buttons

Services should be presented clearly without unnecessary marketing language.

---

## Contact Page

Verify:

- Contact Information
- Branch Information
- Business Hours
- Support Channels
- Contact Form
- Map Integration (if applicable)

Support information should be easy to locate and understand.

---

## Legal Pages

Review:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Security Policy
- Cookie Policy (if applicable)

Legal content should remain clear, readable, and professionally formatted.

---

## Common Improvements

Apply where appropriate:

- Improve spacing.
- Improve typography.
- Improve content hierarchy.
- Improve trust indicators.
- Improve accessibility.
- Improve responsiveness.
- Remove unnecessary visual clutter.
- Remove outdated or repetitive content.
- Maintain consistent branding.

---

## Transformation Checklist

Before approving a public page verify:

- Professional banking appearance achieved.
- Content is accurate and well structured.
- Navigation remains functional.
- Responsive behavior verified.
- Accessibility requirements satisfied.
- Trust indicators clearly presented.
- FEPS standards followed.
- No existing functionality broken.

---

## Completion Criteria

The Public Website Transformation is complete only when every public-facing page reflects a modern enterprise banking experience while remaining fully functional and compliant with FEPS.
