## Purpose
Define the business requirements and product scope for the Finzo System.

---

### Executive Summary
Finzo is a centralized enterprise Banking Management System enabling customer account management, robust administrative governance, staff operations, and secure financial transactions.

### Core Modules

#### 1. Customer Module
- **Registration & KYC:** Customers can register and submit Identity (Aadhaar/PAN) and Address documents for Admin review.
- **Transactions:** Deposit, withdraw, and transfer funds securely.
- **Beneficiaries:** Save account numbers for rapid transfers.
- **Account Management:** View balances and transaction history.

#### 2. Staff Module (Teller Operations)
- **Dashboard:** Access branch-specific transaction statistics.
- **Customer Verification:** Verify identities before initiating over-the-counter transactions.

#### 3. Admin Module
- **User Management:** Approve/Reject KYC, suspend users, and unlock accounts.
- **Staff Management:** Provision branch staff accounts and handle passwords.
- **System Configuration:** Manage global interest rates, transfer limits, and branch configurations.
- **Auditing:** Immutable audit logs tracking every administrative action.
