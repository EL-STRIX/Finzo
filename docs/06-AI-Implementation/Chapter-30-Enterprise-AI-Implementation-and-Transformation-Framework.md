## Part 7 — Enterprise Staff Banking Module Transformation Framework

## Purpose

The Staff Banking Module is the operational layer of the Finzo Banking Management System.

Unlike the Customer Module, which prioritizes customer convenience, the Staff Module prioritizes operational efficiency, accuracy, compliance, accountability, and secure banking operations.

Every staff interface must enable employees to perform banking activities quickly while minimizing operational risk.

The interface should resemble the internal banking systems used by professional financial institutions.

---

## Scope

This framework applies to every staff-facing page, including but not limited to:
